How to read it

Edge is the only column that says bet or don't: model probability minus what the book's price implies. Bar is +4 on shot lines, +6 on anytime goal. Negative edge means the book likes him more than the model does — pass, however good the player.

3+ shots is the main market: calibrated within 2 points in every bucket over 5,000+ graded rows. 2+ is a coin flip books price well. Anytime goal only pays in the +250 to +450 range.

Matchup grades the opponent's shots allowed: A = 29+ (3+ SOG/g forwards hit 3+ shots 70% in October), B = 27–29 (54%), C = under 27 (52%). The model already includes it.

Status: in confirmed in the posted lineup · out not in it · ? didn't dress the team's last game and lineups aren't up yet. Neither out nor ? is ever auto-picked.

Type a book price into any row to see your own edge. Prices you type stay in this browser.
